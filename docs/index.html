The panel, hanging from the notch. Nothing else on screen moves.

Four modes. Only what you want.

Every mode switches off, and so does each piece inside it. Keep the list without the timer. Keep AirDrop without the shelf.

Music

Whatever's playing, right there.

Artwork, a playhead that keeps time between checks, and full transport for Spotify and Apple Music — whichever is actually playing.

  • Shuffle, previous, play, next
  • Nothing polls while both apps are closed
File tray

Park files on the notch.

Drag files up to the notch and the tray opens to catch them. Drag them back out anywhere — or drop them on the AirDrop pad to send them straight on.

  • Files never move or get copied
  • The real system AirDrop sheet
Clipboard

The last twenty things you copied.

One click puts any of them back on your clipboard. Anything your password manager marks private is never recorded.

  • Stays on your Mac, no account
  • Only watched while the mode is on

Two minutes, no terminal.

01

Drag it in

Open the DMG and drag Notchii into Applications.

02

Launch it once

There's no window — look for the mark in your menu bar. Music control asks permission the first time.

03

Hover the notch

That's the whole thing. No notch? Notchii uses a virtual one at the top centre of any display.

Prefer the terminal? git clone the repo and run make install.
