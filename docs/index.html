The notch is a hole
in your screen.
Give it something
to hold.

Hover it and a panel drops out. Your list is in there, so is a tray for files, whatever's playing, and everything you've copied. It closes when you look away.

Download for macOS Apple silicon & Intel · macOS 13+ · 2 MB

Only the parts you actually want.

Four modes, each switched on or off from the gear beside the notch — and every piece inside them too. Keep the list without the timer. Keep AirDrop without the shelf.

Type it, press return, it's on the list.

A timer sits next to it. Type any length — 5, or 5:30 — press return, and it runs.

Skip a track without leaving what you're doing.

Spotify and Apple Music. The panel takes your keystrokes without pulling focus from the app you were in.

Drag a file up. It waits there.

Drag it back out anywhere, or drop it on AirDrop to send it. Your file never moves and never gets copied.

The last twenty things you copied.

Click one to put it back. Anything your password manager marks private is never written down.

Free
No price, no account, no upsellThere is no pro tier and nothing is held back. You will never be asked to pay for a mode.
Lightweight
Two megabytes of native SwiftAppKit and SwiftUI, one universal binary. No Electron, no bundled frameworks, nothing running when the panel is closed.
Configurable
Built out of parts you chooseModes and the pieces inside them switch off independently, so the panel only ever holds what you put in it.
Private
Nothing leaves the machineYour tasks, files and clipboard are files in your own Library folder. Password manager copies are never recorded.
Open source
MIT, every line on GitHubRead it, fork it, or add a mode — each one is about thirty lines.
